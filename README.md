# Food POS

A modern and user-friendly Point of Sale (POS) system designed to simplify restaurant operations and manage daily business activities efficiently.

## Overview

Food POS is a web-based Point of Sale system developed to help restaurants, cafés, and food businesses manage their daily operations through a centralized platform. It provides essential tools for handling customer orders, managing food menus, processing payments, tracking inventory, and monitoring sales.

## Core Features

- **Dashboard** – View sales summaries and overall business activities.
- **Order Management** – Create, update, and manage customer orders.
- **Menu Management** – Add, edit, delete, and organize food and beverage items.
- **Billing & Payments** – Generate customer bills and manage payment transactions.
- **Table Management** – Manage restaurant tables and track order statuses.
- **Inventory Management** – Monitor stock levels and ingredient availability.
- **Sales Reports** – View daily, weekly, and monthly sales information.

## Intended Users

- Restaurants
- Cafés
- Fast-food outlets
- Food shops
- Small and medium-sized food businesses

## Technology Stack

This project is developed using the following web technologies:

- **HTML5** – Used to create the structure of the application.
- **CSS3** – Used for styling, layouts, and responsive design.
- **JavaScript** – Used to implement functionality and interactive features.
